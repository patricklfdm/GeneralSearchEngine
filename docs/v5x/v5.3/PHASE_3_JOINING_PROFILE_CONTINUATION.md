# Phase 3 — selected-cohort continuation and joining adoption

The profile reader now extends [original initialization](PHASE_3_JOINING_PROFILE_INITIALIZATION.md)
through FINAL, the new-view BARRIER, and retaining cold reopen. Its selected
owners come from the independently authenticated SELECTED records. Both old
and new majorities remain required; a valid overlapping majority need not
select the joining owner. The aggregate remains **PARTIAL**.

## Derived continuation

Every declared old-majority owner must supply the original intermediate grant.
PREPARE responses and transferred proposer promises are checked against the
derived grant bytes, permanent identities and correlation fields. Each later
selected grant, installation, application and publication is derived from the
preceding state before comparison with its captured inventory. Unselected
owners retain their original state.

Stable FINAL reconciliation may remove an identity only after proving coverage
of the old plan's obligations. It preserves the exact surviving possible
endpoints, renewal debt and confirmations. JOINT cannot discard an old member,
and a new member cannot appear without the earlier joining-copy derivation.
The historical three-voter ADD entry points retain their default behavior.

## Recovery of an unselected joining owner

In the retained `3-REPLACE` execution, node 1 and node 2 satisfy both governing
majorities. The joining node 4 remains at index 9 while they publish FINAL 10
and BARRIER 11. It then obtains the original final capsule, application image
and ancestry manifest from node 1 through three complete transfers.

The reader binds these transfers to the derived donor's exact bytes and aliases,
after final publication and before the joining adoption. It does not use the
receiver's final manifest or a reference hash as authority. The new generation
inherits the verified local ancestry needed for local retained sources and
peer/learner obligations that the incoming prefix alone does not cover. Every
bridge is checked against original checkpoint and ACK observations.

The expected generation, manifest, selector and optional new promise are
reconstructed. The allocator, retained source, inactive previous generation and
ledger debt survive unchanged. The original local sequence must contain the
promise forces when needed, generation and selector installation, resealing,
and adoption return in order. Final snapshots, immutable custody identities and
the cold-open force sequence are checked separately.

This reader supports the observed newer-authority adoption path. A lagging
joiner that instead needs same-view selected installation is rejected until its
own physical derivation is implemented; the native producer already supports
that path. No claim of full recovery coverage follows from this one history.

## Original-first rejection checks

```bash
python3 -B -m scripts.v53.joining_profile_continuation_evidence /path/to/raw \
  --profile 3-REPLACE --qualify
```

The CLI accepts the five explicit ADD/REPLACE profiles. Conditional profile
tests do not create native evidence for an unexecuted profile. Ten common
negatives cover captured grants, selected inventories, stable ledgers, retained
generations, standalone grants and cold reopen. An original adoption activates
ten additional cases covering local ancestry/debt/source preservation,
allocation, prior generation, diagnostic claims and local force ownership/order.

The untouched original must pass before mutation. Every disposable mutation
must still pass the preceding publication, placement, retirement and
initialization checks, then fail continuation with its exact designated reason.
Changed copied inventories are rehashed; the missing-ancestry case also rewrites
the manifest and selector consistently. Such metadata cannot replace the
independent derivation. Original inventories are checked again after replay.

The first qualification completed 19 negatives, then correctly stopped because
the owner-substitution mutation changed the adoption entry and hit an earlier
recovery boundary. Only that mutation was corrected to target the return record.
The original and the corrected case were replayed; the 19 completed results and
the initial diagnostic remain retained. Validation logic was unchanged, and no
native workload was retried.
