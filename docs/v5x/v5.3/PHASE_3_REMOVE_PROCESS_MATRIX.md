## Indivisible members and pinned readers

The catalogue is exactly two REMOVE profiles, thirteen declared retirement cuts
and halt/SIGKILL. `remove_process_run` executes one new original history in an
isolated recorded source copy. Bootstrap, acquisition, interrupted transaction,
original filesystem recovery and all continuation rounds stay on that worker.
No stage is assembled from a different execution or successful retry.

`remove_process_matrix member` checks the actual retained source files, all three
compiled class inventories, original zero-retry JUnit report, exact Maven command,
original execution record, complete collection and current v2 capture schema.
It independently recollects process observations; `collectionComplete` by itself

diagnostic hash; hosted v2 binds actual tool files and versions while retaining
host-specific diagnostics separately, as described in the hosted entry. The reader hash includes every V5.3
Python module, its V4.3 format dependency and the frozen format catalogue.

The member then runs the complete existing physical/network/export/floor/
retention/retirement/ballot reader and eighteen exact ballot negatives. Those
negatives also require successful continuous retention and retirement replay.
The preceding layers' original-first checks remain mandatory; their earlier
negative qualification is not relabelled as a new matrix execution.

## Aggregate contract

`aggregate` requires the exact 52 directory IDs printed by `plan`. Missing,
additional, failed, aliased or duplicated-original members cannot qualify.
Every member must share the supplied complete source/build/toolchain identity;
each original bootstrap plan must be distinct. The aggregate replays every
member and its eighteen negatives instead of trusting saved member receipts.

Only complete independently bootstrapped histories may run concurrently. Local
replay workers are bounded to 1–4; they cannot create or retry a JVM history.
Failed replay outcomes are retained while other submitted members finish.
Progress is reported at member completion and at most sixty seconds between
polls of ongoing replay work. Every source, class inventory, collection, receipt
and reader binding is rechecked after replay and again before aggregate success.
PID equality across different workers is allowed; the original three distinct
processes are checked within each member's own execution.

Even a successful complete matrix has `allMembershipProfilesQualified=false`,
`originalObservationCoverageComplete=false` and `phase3Complete=false`. Its scope
is the declared REMOVE retirement process matrix through the implemented reader
chain, not the entire frozen Phase 3 acceptance register.
