## Original-first composition

The [composed reader](../../../scripts/v53/automatic_history_replay.py) requires
the entire unmodified physical/network/export/lifecycle chain before any mutation.
It reuses the current restart reducer explicitly, without trying a historical
reader and falling back after failure. Every lower reader's original result must
match the original composition exactly; all originals and attachments are checked
again afterward.

Competition currently recognizes three **derived** shapes: the retained pre-grant
loss with two sources and no partial selection transfer; one local-only grant
lost to a delivered higher-ballot rejection, with three sources; and the superseded
selection with four sources and an incomplete transfer. Classification occurs
after complete original validation. An unknown shape is retained and refused;
it is not simplified, rerun, or assigned the easiest passing catalogue.

The pre-grant shape requires its seven copied-original negatives, twenty export
capability negatives and twelve lifecycle negatives. Seven unavailable export
mutations are explicitly `notApplicable`, never counted as rejected. The
superseded shape requires the full existing prefix, maintenance, publication,
activation, export and lifecycle vectors, including source retirement and the
incomplete transfer. It has no inapplicable export mutation. The exact first
captured owner supplies the prefix reader's owner-specific rejection message.
These paths do not claim arbitrary multiple-round competition coverage.

The local-only-grant branch requires seven original loss/physical negatives,
twenty export negatives and twelve lifecycle negatives, with the same seven
explicitly inapplicable export mutations as the pre-grant branch. A local BASIS
and unanswered PREPAREs never supply a remote vote or installation.

Restart requires 51 current physical/network negatives when an unused source
was actually pulled, or 50 with `current/unused-offer-bytes` explicitly
`notApplicable` when the complete original replay proves none. All 22 attached
lifecycle negatives remain required. Reply revocation requires all 44 original-first negatives
and v2 [resource observations](PHASE_3_TRANSFER_RESOURCE_LIFETIME.md).
The aggregate compares exact ordered case/status/reason vectors, not just counts.
A stage's unrelated failure cannot satisfy its negative expectation.

## Provenance and failure handling

Capture copies the actual checkout once, records source, compiled class inventories,
portable Java/Maven identities, reader hashes and the exact unretried JUnit report.
Subsequent groups can copy that recorded source via `--source-from`. Collection is
bounded inventory and producer-completeness checking only: `status=PARTIAL`,
`nativeHistoryQualified=false`, `phase3Complete=false`.

Admission requires exactly the three distinct producers, 50 distinct original
plans, one source/build/toolchain/reader and the exact native commands. Changed
classes, linked members, extra runs, stale readers, missing attachments and failed
or rerun JUnit reports are rejected. A summary receipt never substitutes for
independent replay. No historical evidence is rebound to the latest commit.

Up to three complete groups can be replayed concurrently. Failure in one group
is retained while the other originals are still inspected. Source, original
inventories and pins are rechecked before returning. CLI progress is emitted at
least once per minute during long replay. Existing output directories are not
overwritten. This entry dispatches no workflow and runs no paid cloud experiment.

## Local commands

not a statement that this development batch ran them:

```bash

OUT=target/v53-automatic-acceptance/manual-001

python3 -B -m scripts.v53.automatic_history capture "$OUT/inputs/competition" \
  --case competition --maven /opt/maven/bin/mvn

  --case higher-ballot-restart --maven /opt/maven/bin/mvn \
```
