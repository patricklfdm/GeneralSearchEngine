## Representation and lifetime

Installed-generation maintenance can retain the complete, already charged local
current generation as its local floor original and physically copy the three
remote originals. The FLOOR record still lists all four original voters and
must satisfy both actual governing majorities. No ownership observation, vote,
quorum member or original image is manufactured or omitted.

`floor-copies/local.gsr` contains the exact existing SELECTOR frame, bounded by
4,096 bytes. It is a private physical sidecar, not a new wire kind or a change to
the frozen record catalog. Its bytes are included in physical inventories and
release authority. Current generation files remain exclusively owned, charged
and immutable until the entire floor is released. No hard link, image alias or
unaccounted duplicate is created. Selected installation, adoption and publication
must reject a generation change while this pin exists.

The automatic maintenance route selects this layout only for an installed
selected generation with an inactive predecessor, at least two retained sources
and at least four offered originals, or when resuming its existing pin. Existing
fully copied floors keep their original representation. Ordinary `establishFloor`
and the capacity rejection reproducer remain unchanged.

The write order is: reserve copied originals, create the floor directory, force
the complete current original and selector, write/force the local selector
sidecar and parents, write/force the proposal, force all remote copies, then
publish/force FLOOR and its parents. An empty directory or exact partial local
selector fixes no quorum and cannot authorize deletion. Once a proposal exists,
recovery retains its exact original cohort. A completed floor requires the full
selector marker; a missing, changed or aliased marker fails closed.

Retirement re-forces both copied and pinned originals before borrowing an exact
image. It removes the predecessor only under the existing durable retirement
record, then removes an obsolete source only after the exact journal replacement.
Release relinquishes FLOOR before deleting copied files, clears the local marker
after the proposal and remote copies, and clears release authority last. It never
lists or deletes the current generation. A later installation is allowed only
after this lifetime ends.

For this conditional two-install fixture, the floor occupies two generations,
two source images and three copied images: **seven images**. Metadata owners are
two sources plus three copied originals, leaving one of the **six SOURCE** slots
for the retirement backup. This is a measured component bound, not qualification
of every simultaneous native transfer/publication maximum.

## Verification and retained limits

preservation, capacity peaks, old generation/source cleanup, cold reopen and the
next installation. It injects exceptions at nine establishment and nine
retirement/release boundaries, resumes exact zero/partial selector prefixes, and
rejects changing, deleting or aliasing the marker and replacing a pinned current.
The handoff regression charges three receiving SOURCE owners, drains each only
after its replacement copy is forced, and resumes an interrupted drain through
individual original-copy recovery before publishing FLOOR.
These reopen tests are same-JVM component recovery; they do not claim SIGKILL or
power-loss qualification.

The independent reader `scripts.v53.pinned_floor_evidence` decodes the four
original seed owners, selected records, source packets and image inventories.
It derives the pinned floor, retired predecessor, compacted source/journal,
released floor and cold snapshots from those inputs, and checks exact force
order. Eleven exact-reason mutations exercise marker/original/peer corruption,
force ordering, capacity, premature source deletion and leftover floor data.
