Phase 3 remains **OPEN**. The original five-to-four native producer has completed
one execution, including BARRIER 8 and DATA 9. This batch extends independent

## Independently derived authority

`scripts.v53.discovered_selection_evidence` first reconstructs the public
bootstrap, seven preparation slots, all prior physical snapshots and both
verified adoptions. It then derives the following original storage:

- Node 4's new stable peer ledger, with only complete original copies from nodes
  4, 1 and 2 narrowing their possible endpoints to cut 7.
- Retirement of the inactive generation and obsolete frozen source, retaining
  the adopted promise through journal rotation before the new allocation.
- Ballot 4, fresh promises and immutable basis packets, and the only selection
  containing nodes 1, 2 and 4. Only fresh source UUIDs are observations; sources,
  votes, merged ledgers, manifests, images and selectors are independently built.
- Every byte and directory of all five owners at the installed snapshot. Nodes
  3 and 5 remain unchanged, including their final retained snapshots.

The network pass binds every response to its owner and original response event.
Within the adoption-to-installation interval it consumes every exchange: complete
same-cut floor packets and metadata, proposer promise, peer basis/promise/packet,
selected record and all selected sources. Fourteen complete source-transfer
traces must precede their dependent floor, grant or selection. The full original
copied images remain required even when a transfer borrows equal local bytes.

Floor authority and copied bytes must be forced before retirement; journal
rotation must be durable before obsolete-source deletion and floor release.
The new grant, selected retention merge and installation must precede their
ACKs. A response merely offered on the wire is not a delivered reply. Up to three
byte-identical attempts may complete one logical exchange; a changed response,
fourth attempt, extra exchange or omitted delivery is rejected. Recovery probes
and the fresh activation keep distinct traces and their respective ballots.

The [source-bound receipt](phase3-evidence/discovered-selection.json) records
the unchanged original archive, reader source inventory and complete results.
The independent reader derives 55 snapshots and all 14 transfers, with 21 exact
negative variants rejected. Twenty-four focused Python checks pass, including
original-first rejection and finite identical-attempt handling. No Java workload,
CI workflow or cloud experiment was rerun for this batch.

The result is deliberately `PARTIAL`, with scope
`original-prelude-adoption-installation`. Its success does not independently
qualify the maintenance, voting, future retention or publication of BARRIER 8
and DATA 9. Later rejected operations by the unselected node 3 are retained,
but this installation reader does not classify their service-time causality.
Those records, the complete post-installation history, remaining membership

The existing prelude/adoption reader keeps its original output and limitations;
it now exposes its already reduced originals for this additional reader. There
is no production Java, frozen format, workload limit, retry policy, POM or
workflow change. Rollback removes the new reader/tests and restores the private
