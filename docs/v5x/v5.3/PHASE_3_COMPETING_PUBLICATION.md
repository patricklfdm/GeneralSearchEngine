## Original network and bounded failures

The publication interval replays **175** non-heartbeat exchanges and **35**
complete transfers. Votes, proofs, checkpoint acknowledgements and future
retention confirmations must arrive from their original owners before dependent
forces. Ten heartbeat samples have twenty replies and ten majority contacts.

The previous single-campaign reader calculated future-intent sequence numbers
as if the proposer were the first sorted node. Production
`MembershipFutureRetentionQuorum.record` reserves sequence 1 for the local
voter, then iterates the sorted remote voters. The shared reader now follows
that rule. The retained run's proposer is node-3; no production fix or message
rewrite is involved. Regressions exercise all three proposer positions.

Two unused future-intent requests and three unsuccessful checkpoint pulls are
explicitly accounted for. `MembershipPublicationEndpoint.collect` first tries
the sorted eligible owner; node-1 tries node-2 before node-3. For each relevant
original current/parent statement, the reader derives node-2's exact expected
CHECKPOINT_ACK digest and requires three identical initial requests, no receipt,
fresh transfer identities, and the later successful original-peer pull after
exhaustion. These failed requests supply **no** quorum vote, bytes or ownership.
Arbitrary unanswered CHUNKs cannot pass this exception. Both original selected
owners must still have their successful complete transfers.

The shared single-campaign entry retains its stricter one-round/nonwriting
requirements. The new continuation derives outgoing scratch owners from the
original local grants, including the losing proposer. Every original owner must
close with zero leases and exactly empty incoming/outgoing transfer contents.
Diagnostic READY/loss rows are checked for consistency but never grant authority.
